# Research and teaching boundary

The original local research workspace describes independent ability/skill coding, agreement measurement before reconciliation, and human ratification of a versioned rubric. The course conversion does not attest that this process is complete.

The teaching repository includes narrative readings and course exercises. It excludes local raw exports, research handoff records, draft papers, third-party research checkouts, and ongoing media-production state. Lesson 14 uses synthetic examples; its mappings are not the frozen research rubric. No research approval or employment finding is inferred from course completion.
