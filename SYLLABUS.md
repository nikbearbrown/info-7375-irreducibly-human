# INFO 7375 — Irreducibly Human

Fall 2026. **Predict → Build It → Use It → Ship It → Verify**.

## Course description

Students investigate the division of work between people and AI across pattern recognition, embodied activity, social interaction, supervision, causal reasoning, collective judgment, and practical wisdom. They build small Python mechanisms, apply them with Claude Code, and test the resulting claims. The book's taxonomy provides a framework to examine; its strongest capability and philosophical claims are not conclusions students must accept to earn credit.

## Outcomes

- Separate a human-capacity taxonomy from a dated claim about machine capability.
- Measure when an intervention or override changes a baseline result.
- Preserve enough understanding to check work delegated to AI.
- Distinguish simulated interaction from actual observation, consent, or relationship.
- Compute calibration and causal toy examples while stating their assumptions.
- Explain how correlated errors affect aggregation and preserve dissent in summaries.
- Examine normative choices separately from numerical optimization.
- Deliver an evidence-backed division of labor with identifiable AI and human contributions.

## Method and reading

Predict before observing the result. Build a small Python mechanism. Use it on a concrete case with Claude Code. Ship an inspectable candidate. Verify the packaged result and revise where necessary. Every lesson states **AI should** and **Human should** with specific actions. Human judgment also requires evidence; it is not assumed infallible.

The [fifteen-lesson map](README.md) expands seven tiers plus introductory and concluding material into practice. [Companion references](docs/reading-map.md) connect Computational Skepticism, Conducting AI, Prompt Engineering, and Branding and AI where they add a distinct check or supervisory practice.

## Assessment

Lesson exercises and knowledge checks are **ungraded Assessments**. Ten [Assignments](assignments/fall-2026/README.md) occur every ten days; Canvas supplies actual deadlines. Each totals 100 points: 60 implementation, 10 Frictional, 10 GitHub version posting, 20 Relative Quartile. Explainers are optional within communication quality and have no separate points.

An honest wrong prediction, unsuccessful attempt, or supported disagreement can earn appropriate credit. Do not invent a struggle or real participant to complete an exercise. The implementation points evaluate the requested Python mechanism and the reasoning supported by it. Frictional records actual effort; GitHub points assess delivery; Relative Quartile compares resulting quality.

Submit matching Canvas and GitHub versions under fall-2026/first-name-last-initial/assignment-XX/, with the final commit hash in Canvas. The final capstone integrates at least three relevant tiers around a bounded working artifact.

## Research boundary

The local exposure research has a documented pending human rubric ratification. The course uses synthetic data and explicitly illustrative mappings for its core index exercise. Students do not sign that research gate, change research tags, or present classroom results as validated employment forecasts. The ability-to-tier research coding rubric is distinct from the Assignment grading rubric.
